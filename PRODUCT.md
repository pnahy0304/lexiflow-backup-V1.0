# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

adaptive

## Stack

Flutter 3.x with Dart, Firebase Core/Auth, Cloudflare D1 & Workers, Google ML Kit Text Recognition, Audioplayers, Google Fonts.

## Users

English language learners, students, and professionals in Vietnam who want an Oxford-grade dictionary with TFLAT Vietnamese translations, camera OCR reading, and SM-2 spaced repetition flashcards for efficient vocabulary retention.

## Product Purpose

LexiFlow combines accurate Oxford definitions, instant TFLAT Vietnamese translations, camera OCR instant lookup, and an SM-2 spaced repetition flashcard engine to turn daily vocabulary lookup into long-term memory.

## Positioning

The only Vietnamese English learning app that unifies Oxford phonetic & definitions, TFLAT Vietnamese context, camera document scanner, and SM-2 algorithm-driven daily reviews in a modern, dark-mode ready mobile experience.

## Operating Context

Used on mobile devices during daily learning, reading physical or digital books/articles via camera OCR, reviewing flashcards on the go, and taking quick vocabulary quizzes.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Instant Oxford & TFLAT vocabulary lookup with audio pronunciations.
- SM-2 Spaced Repetition Flashcard system (Again, Hard, Good, Easy ratings).
- Camera OCR scanner for live text-to-word translation.
- Interactive multiple-choice vocabulary quizzes.
- Search history & Bookmarking backed by Cloudflare D1.
- Full Light & Dark mode support.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: LexiFlow
- Tone: Educational, modern, encouraging, tactile, and clear.
- Palette: Royal Indigo primary, Pastel Soft Slate surface, Vibrant Amber accent, Emerald success, Coral red error.

## Product Principles

1. Tactile & Delightful: Interactions should feel responsive, warm, and visually engaging.
2. Cognitive Clarity: Information architecture prioritizes quick lookup, clean phonetic display, and distraction-free learning.
3. Universal Dark/Light Harmony: All screens and cards seamlessly adapt to Light and Dark themes without visual defects or harsh contrasts.
